# Claim Boundaries

## Allowed

- Public portfolio repositories and conservatively stated professional evidence supplied for this case.
- Proposed methods, research questions and potential interventions with explicit labels.
- Transferability claims that preserve the direct legal-practice gap.

## Excluded

No Reed Smith employee, attorney or client interviews; no internal workflows, documents, pain points, metrics or observations; no implementation results; no unsubstantiated legal-services expertise; no completed law-firm ethnography; no law-firm tenure; no completed degree claim.

## Epistemic order

PUBLIC EVIDENCE → OBSERVATION → INTERPRETATION → HYPOTHESIS → RESEARCH QUESTION → PROPOSED METHOD → POTENTIAL INTERVENTION.

A hypothesis is never a finding. A proposed intervention is never an outcome. An inferred need is never an observed fact.

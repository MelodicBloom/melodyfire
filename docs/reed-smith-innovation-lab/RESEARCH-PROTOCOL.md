# Proposed Research Protocol

**Status: proposed method, not internal Reed Smith findings.**

1. Frame the decision, stakeholders, constraints, unknowns, assumptions, access, confidentiality and success.
2. Observe through interviews, contextual observation, walkthroughs, artifacts and decision-point mapping.
3. Synthesize traceable records, clusters, tensions, workarounds, uncertainty and opportunities.
4. Co-design from evidence and constraints, not technology.
5. Prioritize desirability, client value, feasibility, judgment, risk, confidence, reversibility and learning.
6. Pilot with an owner, hypothesis, evidence basis, human boundary, measures, review and stop/scale conditions.

## Reframing rules

1. **Preserve canonical project names.** Use a subtitle rather than a wholesale renaming unless Jennipher explicitly approves a name change.
2. **Lead with function, not aesthetic label.** The descriptor should explain what a buyer is looking at in a single sentence.
3. **Separate phase from value.** “In development” does not mean weak; it means a public page must state present evidence and next gate rather than future capability as fact.
4. **Make contribution explicit.** A project cannot serve as professional proof until Jennipher’s contribution, collaborators, and boundaries are described accurately.
5. **Use public-safe evidence.** Private repos, third-party clients, and sensitive information remain inaccessible even when they are useful internally.
6. **Do not infer maturity from commits or visual polish.** “Live,” “pilot,” “deployed,” outcomes, and client use are human-gated labels.

## Executive result

The two Melodyfire packages are substantially consistent, but they represent different lifecycle states. The **v4 package** is the structured review mandate, protocol, seed inventory, schemas, contracts, and reference blueprint. The **Details package** is a later delivery bundle containing the completed 15-file independent review, evidence log, mobile captures, selected source snapshots, and a nested copy of the completed review archive. The later bundle is therefore a **delivery artifact built from the v4 package**, not a competing v4 specification.

The central conclusion is unchanged across both: establish canonical project identity, evidence, semantic detail routes, and mobile baseline correctness before expanding interaction complexity; use editorial-first Work with an optional, measured spatial Explore mode; resolve deployment/Supabase authority before CMS, AI, or publication changes.
